My Narration Process

  1. 01

    Manuscript review

    You send your book. I read it for tone, characters, and pronunciation.

  2. 02

    Narration

    We agree on style, then I record your story in my own voice.

  3. 03

    Audio production

    Careful editing, cleanup, and level matching for a polished result.

  4. 04

    Final delivery

    You receive finished audio files, ready to review and publish.
